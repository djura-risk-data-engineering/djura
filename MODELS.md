# Supported ground motion and correlation models

This page lists every ground motion model (GMM/GMPE) and every intensity
measure (IM) correlation model bundled with **djura**, together with the
scientific publication each one implements.

- GMMs live in [`src/djura/record_selection/gsim/models/`](https://github.com/djura-risk-data-engineering/djura/blob/main/src/djura/record_selection/gsim/models/)
  and are re-exported from `djura.record_selection.gsim.models`.
- Correlation models live in
  [`correlation_models.py`](https://github.com/djura-risk-data-engineering/djura/blob/main/src/djura/record_selection/correlation_models.py) (horizontal),
  [`correlation_models_v.py`](https://github.com/djura-risk-data-engineering/djura/blob/main/src/djura/record_selection/correlation_models_v.py) (vertical / H–V),
  and are dispatched through
  [`correlations.py`](https://github.com/djura-risk-data-engineering/djura/blob/main/src/djura/record_selection/correlations.py).

Many GMM implementations are adapted from the
[OpenQuake Engine](https://github.com/gem/oq-engine) (GEM Foundation,
AGPL-3.0-or-later); see `NOTICE.md` for the full attribution.

Intensity measure abbreviations used below: `PGA`, `PGV`, `PGD` (peak ground
acceleration/velocity/displacement), `SA` (5 %-damped spectral acceleration),
`AVGSA` (`Sa_avg`, average spectral acceleration), `SA_vert` (vertical
spectral acceleration), `IA` (Arias intensity), `CAV` (cumulative absolute
velocity), `RSD575`/`RSD595`/`RSD2080` (`Ds5-75`, `Ds5-95`, `Ds20-80`
relative significant duration), `FIV3` (filtered incremental velocity).

## 2. Correlation models

Correlation models return the correlation coefficient between the residuals
(epsilons) of two intensity measures. The registry of supported IM pairs and
model names is `CORRELATION_MODELS` in
[`constants.py`](https://github.com/djura-risk-data-engineering/djura/blob/main/src/djura/record_selection/constants.py).

### 2.2 Next-generation IM correlations (ANN-based)

`aso2024` is the single ANN-based model covering the widest set of IM pairs.
It is registered for: `SA-SA`, `SA-Sa_avg2`, `SA-Sa_avg3`, `SA-FIV3`,
`SA-Ds575`, `SA-Ds595`, `Sa_avg-Sa_avg`, `Sa_avg2-Sa_avg2`,
`Sa_avg2-Sa_avg3`, `Sa_avg3-Sa_avg3`, `Sa_avg2-PGA`, `Sa_avg3-PGA`,
`Sa_avg2-PGV`, `Sa_avg3-PGV`, `Sa_avg2-Ds575`, `Sa_avg2-Ds595`,
`Sa_avg3-Ds575`, `Sa_avg3-Ds595`, `Sa_avg2-FIV3`, `FIV3-FIV3`, `FIV3-PGA`,
`FIV3-PGV`, `FIV3-Ds575`, `FIV3-Ds595` and `FIV3-Sa_avg3`.

> Aristeidou, S., Shahnazaryan, D., & O'Reilly, G. J. (2024). Correlation
> models for next-generation amplitude and cumulative intensity measures
> using artificial neural networks. *Earthquake Spectra*.
> DOI: [10.1177/87552930241270563](https://doi.org/10.1177/87552930241270563)

A legacy tabulated variant of the same family is available as `ann_corr`
(reads `assets/correlation_models.json`); `aso2024` evaluates the trained
network directly and is the model exposed through the registry.

Because the total correlation blends the two using the standard-deviation
split of the companion vertical GMPE (`GulerceEtAl2017`), it additionally
requires the magnitude and region. The implementation is adapted from the
MATLAB code of N. S. Kwong.

### 2.8 Horizontal-to-vertical (H–V) correlation

`rho_HV_indirect` derives the H–V correlation `rho_{H,V}(Ti, Tj)` from a
horizontal GMM, a V/H GMM, a horizontal SA–SA correlation model and an
H-to-V/H cross-correlation model, avoiding the need for a direct H–V model
and extending the usable period range beyond that of Baker & Cornell (2006).

> Kwong, N. S., & Chopra, A. K. (2020). Selecting, scaling, and orienting
> three components of ground motions for intensity-based assessments at
> far-field sites. *Earthquake Spectra*, 36(3).
> DOI: [10.1177/8755293019899954](https://doi.org/10.1177/8755293019899954)

Typical component models for its four inputs are Campbell & Bozorgnia (2014)
(`sigma_H`), Bozorgnia & Campbell (2016) V/H (`sigma_V/H`), Baker & Jayaram
(2008) (`rho_H,H`) and Gulerce & Abrahamson (2011) (`rho_H,V/H`) — all
available in djura.
